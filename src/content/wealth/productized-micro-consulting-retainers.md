## The practical answer

Pick a problem you can explain in one sentence and solve repeatedly. Offer a fixed deliverable—such as a website accessibility review, monthly bookkeeping check, or onboarding audit—instead of vague “strategy.” Define what the client receives, what they must provide, the timeline, revisions, communication channel, and what is outside scope.

Pilot the package with one or two clients and record the actual hours, delays, and questions. Price for delivery time, admin, taxes, tools, and a profit margin; do not copy an internet rate card. A retainer should describe recurring work and an exit process, not promise unlimited access.

Use a written agreement, clear payment dates, and change orders for extra work. Ask for permission before using client results as a case study. A small service is healthier when it is repeatable and honest about outcomes—not when it disguises uncertainty with business jargon.


## The Structural Flaw of Hourly Billing

Trading time directly for currency represents a fundamental ceiling on solo advisory income. When you charge an hourly rate, efficiency is financially penalized: solving a client's critical revenue bottleneck in two hours yields 75% less compensation than taking eight sluggish hours to reach the identical outcome.

Furthermore, hourly invoicing invites micro-auditing of timesheets, shifts client attention from **business outcomes** to **billable minutes**, and produces erratic cash flow characterized by feast-and-famine cycles.

**Productized micro-consulting** fundamentally changes this dynamic. By defining an unambiguous outcome, setting rigid delivery boundaries, and offering recurring advisory retainers, solo operators achieve predictable monthly recurring revenue (MRR) with minimal administrative overhead.

## Anatomy of a Productized Micro-Consulting Offering

A successful productized consulting package satisfies three structural constraints:

1. **A Narrow, High-Impact Bottleneck:** You do not offer "general digital marketing" or "business strategy." You offer "SaaS Onboarding Funnel Optimization" or "Shopify Checkout Velocity Audits."
2. **Standardized Delivery Rhythm:** Instead of custom proposals, every client receives a fixed workflow (e.g., Week 1 Diagnostic Audit, Week 2 Strategy Roadmap, Weeks 3–4 Implementation Oversight).
3. **Bounded Asynchronous Communication:** Clients receive a weekly 45-minute strategic review call and asynchronous messaging via Slack or Loom with a defined 24-hour response SLA.

## The Three-Tier Retainer Architecture

| Tier | Format & Scope | Target Client | Monthly Pricing Benchmark |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1: Strategic Advisory** | Bi-weekly 45-min consultation + async review of client work (max 3 documents/mo). | Early-stage founders needing directional guidance. | $1,500 – $2,500 / month |
| **Tier 2: Embedded Sprint Partner** | Weekly sprint call + hands-on architecture / review + dedicated Slack channel. | Growth-stage businesses executing key transitions. | $3,500 – $5,000 / month |
| **Tier 3: Fractional Specialization** | Bi-weekly sprint delivery + weekly leadership sync + priority turnaround. | Established firms requiring specialized interim expertise. | $6,500 – $10,000 / month |

## Client Acquisition Without Cold Outbound Spam

Securing 3 to 5 high-value advisory retainers does not require broad influencer distribution. It requires deep, undeniable domain authority in front of decision-makers:

### The "Tear-Down" Methodology
Identify three visible friction points in a prospective client's public operations (e.g., a broken mobile checkout flow, a poorly indexed SEO directory, an ambiguous SaaS pricing page). Record a concise, 5-minute Loom video breaking down:
- The exact friction mechanism.
- The measurable revenue or retention loss it causes.
- The three-step protocol to resolve it.

Deliver this video with zero sales pitch: *"I noticed this specific friction in your checkout flow and put together a 5-minute video detailing how we fixed this for a similar product. Hope it's useful to your engineering team."* This consultative approach converts inbound trust at significantly higher rates than cold pitch templates.
