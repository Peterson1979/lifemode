## The 4-Step Tool Selection Rubric

When deciding whether to integrate a new AI tool into your regular stack, run it through four practical tests:

1. **Context Window Fidelity:** Does the model retain details from the beginning of a 50-page document, or does it hallucinate midway through?
2. **Data Privacy &amp; Governance:** Does the platform offer strict enterprise data opt-outs that guarantee your confidential data is not used to train future public models?
3. **Export &amp; Artifact Flexibility:** Can you easily export generated outputs into clean Markdown, JSON, SVG, or executable code without manual reformatting?
4. **Integration Ecosystem:** Does the tool connect via API, browser extension, or webhooks to the systems you already use daily?

## Summary Recommendation

Do not pay for multiple overlapping $20/month chatbot subscriptions. Select one primary conversational workspace that matches your dominant cognitive task—ChatGPT for multimodal versatility or Claude for document-dense reasoning—and master its keyboard shortcuts and prompting capabilities before adding specialized niche tools.
