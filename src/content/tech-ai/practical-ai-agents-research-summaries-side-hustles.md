The landscape of online side businesses is shifting rapidly from manual labor to **agentic AI systems**.

An AI agent differs from a simple chatbot: instead of answering a single prompt, an agent is given an overarching goal, breaks it into subtasks, queries external tools or web search APIs, evaluates intermediate findings, and compiles a comprehensive deliverable autonomously.

For independent professionals and creators, this creates the opportunity to build **specialized AI-powered micro-businesses** that deliver high-value research and content intelligence to corporate clients with minimal manual overhead.

## Core Building Blocks of an AI Research Agent

To build a reliable agentic workflow, you orchestrate four foundational components:

1. **Planner &amp; Task Decomposer:** Breaks a broad request (e.g., *"Analyze recent solar patent filings"*) into targeted search queries.
2. **Tool Execution Layer:** Executes live web queries, PDF document extraction, and database lookups.
3. **Synthesis &amp; Evaluation Engine:** Evaluates retrieved sources for credibility, cross-references claims, and flags contradictions.
4. **Human-in-the-Loop Quality Gate:** A brief 5-minute human editorial review before final delivery to ensure perfect domain accuracy.
