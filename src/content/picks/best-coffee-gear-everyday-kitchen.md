## Tips for Better Foam Texture

- **Optimal Temperature:** Heat milk to between 140°F and 155°F (60°C–68°C). Overheating degrades milk proteins and destroys sweetness.
- **Angle and Depth:** Submerge the frother whisk head at a 45-degree angle just below the surface until a subtle vortex forms, then lower slightly to incorporate the microfoam evenly.
