## Understanding Heart Rate Intensity Zones

Training in Zone 2—often called "easy cardio"—builds the mitochondrial and cardiovascular foundation necessary for all-day energy and long-term metabolic health:

1. **Cellular Energy Production:** At 60% to 70% of maximum heart rate (or heart rate reserve), your muscle cells recruit Type I slow-twitch fibers that rely on oxygen and fat oxidation rather than rapid glycogen depletion.
2. **The Conversational Talk Test:** Zone 2 is an intensity where you can speak 2 to 3 complete sentences out loud without gasping for breath, allowing your cardiovascular system to adapt without next-day joint soreness or systemic fatigue.
3. **Cardiovascular Baseline:** The American Heart Association (AHA) and CDC recommend at least 150 minutes of moderate aerobic conditioning per week to maintain optimal arterial compliance and blood pressure.
