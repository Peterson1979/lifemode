## Fundamentals of Food Preservation & Cold Chain Control

Food degradation occurs via three primary mechanisms: microbial proliferation (bacteria, molds, yeasts), enzymatic breakdown, and chemical oxidation.

### The USDA "Danger Zone" Rule
Bacterial growth accelerates rapidly between **4°C and 60°C (40°F and 140°F)**. Pathogenic bacteria such as *Bacillus cereus*, *Salmonella*, and *Staphylococcus aureus* can double in population every 20 minutes in this range.

### Refrigerator Temperature Zones
* **Upper & Middle Shelves (3–4°C / 37–40°F):** Most stable temperatures; ideal for dairy, cooked leftovers, and deli items.
* **Bottom Shelf (1–2°C / 34–36°F):** Coldest zone; always reserve for raw proteins (poultry, beef, fish) in sealed trays.
* **Door Bins (5–8°C / 41–46°F):** High temperature fluctuation due to door opening; use for condiments and pasteurized juices, never for fresh milk or eggs.
