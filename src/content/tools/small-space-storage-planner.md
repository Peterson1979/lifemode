## Principles of Small-Space Spatial Organization

Maximizing storage in small apartments and compact homes requires thinking in three dimensions rather than relying solely on floor space:

1. **Vertical Air Rights:** The space between top closet shelves and the ceiling often goes unused. Utilizing structured, dust-sealed fabric storage totes (100L capacity) protects off-season coats and extra duvets without cluttering active living areas.
2. **Double-Hanging Multipliers:** Dividing a standard 80-inch closet height into dual rods (upper for shirts/blouses, lower for trousers) doubles hanging capacity from 12 to 24 garments per linear foot.
3. **Point-of-Use Accessibility:** Store high-frequency daily essentials between eye and hip level, reserving low floor bins and high overhead shelves for seasonal or backup items.
