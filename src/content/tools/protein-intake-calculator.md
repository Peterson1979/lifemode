## Why Per-Meal Protein Distribution Matters

Reaching your optimal daily protein intake is essential for cellular repair, immune health, metabolic rate, and muscle preservation as you age.

However, clinical nutrition research shows that **how you distribute protein throughout the day is just as critical as total daily intake**:

1. **The Leucine Trigger:** To stimulate muscle protein synthesis (MPS), your body requires roughly 2.5 to 3 grams of the essential amino acid leucine in a single sitting—typically provided by 25 to 35 grams of complete protein.
2. **Daytime Fullness & Satiety:** Spreading protein evenly across breakfast, lunch, and dinner prevents mid-morning energy crashes and reduces nighttime carbohydrate cravings.
3. **Age-Related Protection:** As we age, our muscles experience subtle anabolic resistance, making per-meal protein anchoring an effective habit for maintaining strength and physical independence.
