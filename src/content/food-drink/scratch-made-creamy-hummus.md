## About This Dish

Silky smooth homemade hummus with chickpeas, creamy tahini, fresh lemon juice, crushed garlic, and extra virgin olive oil.

## Culinary Notes & Technique

The secret to ultra-smooth hummus is whipping tahini and lemon juice with ice-cold water before blending in the cooked chickpeas.

## Ingredients

- 1 can (15 oz) cooked chickpeas (or 200g dry soaked chickpeas)
- 1/2 teaspoon baking soda (if cooking chickpeas from scratch)
- 1/2 cup quality tahini paste
- 1/4 cup fresh lemon juice (approx. 1.5 to 2 lemons)
- 1 to 2 cloves fresh garlic, roughly chopped
- 1/4 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1/4 teaspoon za'atar (optional)
- 1 tablespoon extra virgin olive oil, plus more for serving
- 2 to 3 tablespoons ice-cold water
- Fine sea salt to taste
- Fresh flat-leaf parsley and ground sumac for garnish

## Instructions

1. If using canned chickpeas, simmer them for 15–20 minutes with a pinch of baking soda until very soft, then drain and rinse with cold water.
2. Combine the chopped garlic and fresh lemon juice with a pinch of salt in a food processor. Pulse finely and let rest 10 minutes to mellow raw garlic sharpness.
3. Add tahini paste to the food processor and blend until thick and creamy.
4. With the food processor running on high, slowly drizzle in 2 to 3 tablespoons of ice-cold water. Blend until the tahini mixture becomes exceptionally pale, fluffy, and smooth.
5. Add the warm drained chickpeas and ground cumin. Process on high for 2 to 3 minutes, drizzling in 1 tablespoon of olive oil.
6. Taste and adjust salt and lemon juice as needed. For extra lightness, add another splash of ice water while blending.
7. Transfer to a shallow bowl, create a swirl with the back of a spoon, drizzle with extra virgin olive oil, and dust with sumac or za'atar.
