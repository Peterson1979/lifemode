## About This Dish

An Italian baked pasta al forno layered with tender broccoli florets, creamy béchamel sauce, and melted smoked scamorza cheese.

## Culinary Notes & Technique

Smoked scamorza imparts an earthy, distinctive note that elevates the sweetness of tender broccoli and velvety béchamel.

## Ingredients

- 500g short pasta (such as rigatoni, paccheri, or penne)
- 500g fresh broccoli florets
- 4 large farm eggs, hard-boiled and sliced
- 200g smoked scamorza (or provolone) cheese, diced
- 400g classic silky béchamel sauce
- 1/3 cup freshly grated Parmigiano-Reggiano
- Fresh sage leaves (optional)
- Coarse sea salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste

## Instructions

1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a rolling boil. Add pasta and cook until very al dente (about 3 minutes less than package directions). Drain and toss with a drop of olive oil.
2. In a separate pot, blanch broccoli florets in boiling salted water for 6–8 minutes until tender-crisp. Drain well.
3. Preheat oven to 180°C (350°F). Lightly butter a 9x13-inch baking casserole dish.
4. Spread a thin foundational layer of béchamel sauce across the bottom of the dish.
5. Add half the pasta, followed by half the broccoli florets, sliced hard-boiled eggs, and diced scamorza cheese.
6. Pour over half the remaining béchamel and season with salt and pepper.
7. Repeat with the remaining pasta, broccoli, eggs, scamorza, and top with the rest of the béchamel sauce.
8. Scatter grated Parmigiano-Reggiano and fresh sage leaves across the top.
9. Bake at 180°C for 15–20 minutes until bubbling, then switch to the broiler for 5 minutes until the top is golden and caramelized.
