## About This Dish

A deeply aromatic sourdough loaf packed with pre-soaked whole rye grains, toasted sesame, sunflower seeds, and golden flax.

## Culinary Notes & Technique

Soaking whole seeds and grain berries overnight ensures they stay moist during baking and prevents them from drawing hydration away from the bread crumb.

## Ingredients

- 400g active mature sourdough starter
- 300g unbleached wheat bread flour
- 200g whole rye flour
- 200g warm water (25–35°C)
- 20g fine sea salt
- 2 teaspoons bread spices (coriander, caraway, fennel seeds)
- 100g whole grain berries (rye, spelt, or wheat berries)
- 40g flaxseed / linseed
- 30g raw sesame seeds
- 30g raw sunflower seeds

## Instructions

1. The day before: Simmer the 100g grain berries in 500g water for 45 minutes until tender. In a separate bowl, soak flaxseed, sesame, and sunflower seeds in 100g water. Cover and let both sit overnight (12–16 hours).
2. Next day: Drain any unabsorbed water from the seeds and cooked grain berries.
3. In a large mixing bowl, combine the mature sourdough starter, warm water, wheat flour, rye flour, sea salt, bread spices, and the soaked seed mixture.
4. Knead thoroughly for 8–10 minutes until a cohesive, extensible dough forms.
5. Shape into a round boule or batard and place in a floured proofing basket. Cover and let ferment in a warm spot (25–30°C) for 4 to 5 hours until noticeably aerated.
6. Preheat Dutch oven inside oven to 250°C (480°F).
7. Turn the dough onto parchment, score the top with a sharp baker's lame, and transfer into the hot Dutch oven. Cover and bake with trapped steam for 20 minutes.
8. Remove the lid, lower the oven temperature to 150°C (300°F), and bake for another 50–60 minutes until crust is dark mahogany and sounds hollow when tapped on the base.
9. Allow the loaf to cool completely on a wire rack for at least 2 hours before slicing.
