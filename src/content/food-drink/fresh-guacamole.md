## About This Dish

A classic, fresh guacamole made with ripe Hass avocados, lime juice, diced onion, tomato, and fresh cilantro.

## Culinary Notes & Technique

Best consumed fresh, as avocado oxidizes quickly when exposed to air. The lime juice provides acid balance while helping retain vibrant green color.

## Ingredients

- 2 ripe Hass avocados
- 1/4 medium onion, finely minced
- 1 small Roma tomato, minced
- Fresh cilantro, chopped
- 1 whole fresh lime
- Coarse sea salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste

## Instructions

1. Cut the avocados in half, remove the pit, and scoop the pulp into a bowl.
2. Mash the avocados with a fork, add freshly squeezed lime juice, mix and mash until desired texture is reached.
3. Mince the cilantro, tomato, and onion, then fold them into the avocado mixture.
4. Season with sea salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste, mixing thoroughly.
5. Enjoy immediately with warm corn totopos or fresh sliced vegetables.
