## The FIFO Rule and Vertical Space Maximization

The most common mistake in pantry organization is treating shelves as single horizontal planes without utilizing vertical cubic volume.

* **Tiered Risers:** Use 3-tier stepped risers for canned goods so label text is always visible.
* **Airtight Decanting:** Commercial paper bags allow moisture exchange and weevil penetration. Clear rectangular airtight containers maximize shelf depth and protect grains from oxidation.
