## Building Longevity Through Systematic Inspection

Homes deteriorate not from catastrophic events, but from small, unaddressed water leaks and thermal inefficiencies over time.

By dedicating 90 minutes each quarter to this systematic checklist, homeowners protect equity, reduce monthly heating and cooling bills by up to 15%, and avoid the stressful panic of emergency trade calls during extreme weather events.
