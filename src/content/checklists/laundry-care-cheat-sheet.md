## Why Less Detergent Produces Cleaner Clothes

Modern high-efficiency (HE) washing machines use minimal water. Adding excess detergent creates an overabundance of suds that cushions clothes from the mechanical rubbing required to lift dirt, while leaving behind a microscopic residue that turns whites dingy and causes garments to smell musty.
