## Practical Kitchen Sanitation Best Practices

A clean kitchen is not merely aesthetic—it is your home's primary defense against foodborne pathogens such as *Listeria monocytogenes*, *Salmonella*, and *Norovirus*.

### The Principle of Top-to-Bottom Cleaning
Always clean from the highest point (top of refrigerator, upper cabinet tops, range hood) down to countertops, sink, and finally the floor. Dust and debris fall downward; cleaning the floor first results in redundant work.

### Avoiding Chemical Cross-Reactions
* **Never mix bleach and vinegar:** Creates toxic chlorine gas ($Cl_2$).
* **Never mix bleach and ammonia:** Produces toxic chloramine vapor ($NH_2Cl$).
* **Safe natural cleansers:** White distilled vinegar (acidic for mineral scale) and Baking soda (mild abrasive base for grease) used in sequence.
