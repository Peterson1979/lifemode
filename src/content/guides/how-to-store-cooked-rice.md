## The Microbiology of Bacillus Cereus

Raw rice naturally harbors bacterial endospores of *Bacillus cereus*, a soil-dwelling microorganism. 

Standard boiling and rice cooker temperatures (100°C / 212°F) kill competing vegetative microbes but do not destroy heat-resistant endospores. Rapid cooling is the only mechanism that prevents these dormant spores from germinating into toxin-producing bacteria.
