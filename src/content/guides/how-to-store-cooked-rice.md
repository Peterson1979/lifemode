## The Microbiology of Bacillus Cereus

Raw rice naturally harbors bacterial endospores of *Bacillus cereus*, a soil-dwelling bacterium. 

Normal boiling and rice cooker temperatures (100°C / 212°F) kill competing vegetative microorganisms but do not destroy heat-resistant *B. cereus* endospores.

```
Raw Rice (Contains Spores) → Boiling Water (Spores Survive)
                                       ↓
         Room Temp Cooling (20–40°C) → Spores Germinate → Toxin (Cereulide) Formed
                                       ↓
         Rapid Refrigerator Cooling (<4°C) → Spores Remain Dormant (SAFE)
```

By cooling cooked rice to below 4°C within 1 hour, spores are trapped in their dormant state and cannot replicate or synthesize toxins.
