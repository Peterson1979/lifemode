## Sizing Calculation Formula

```
Household Size × 4 to 6 cu. ft. + 4 cu. ft. Reserve = Recommended Total Capacity

Examples:
• 1 Person:   (1 × 5) + 4 = ~10–14 cu. ft. (Compact/Apartment)
• 2 People:   (2 × 5) + 4 = ~14–18 cu. ft. (Standard Small)
• 3–4 People: (4 × 5) + 4 = ~20–24 cu. ft. (Standard Family)
• 5+ People:  (5 × 5) + 4 = ~26–30 cu. ft. (Extra Large)
```
