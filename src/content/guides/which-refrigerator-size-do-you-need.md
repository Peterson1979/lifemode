## Sizing Guidelines & Practical Planning

When selecting refrigerator capacity, factor in household shopping frequency, meal preparation habits, and seasonal entertaining. 

Always ensure adequate clearance around the appliance cavity (at least 1/2 inch on the sides and 1 inch on the top and rear) to maintain compressor airflow, energy efficiency, and motor longevity.
