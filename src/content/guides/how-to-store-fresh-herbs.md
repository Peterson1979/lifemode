## The Biology of Herb Respiration and Water Loss

Culinary herbs are living plant tissues that continue cellular respiration after harvest:

$$\text{Glucose } (C_6H_{12}O_6) + 6O_2 \rightarrow 6CO_2 + 6H_2O + \text{Energy}$$

* **Transpiration Loss:** Through microscopic stomata on leaf surfaces, water vapor evaporates into dry refrigerator air (typically 30–40% relative humidity). Without a water source, the stem loses turgor pressure, resulting in limp, wilted leaves within 48 hours.
* **The "Flower Bouquet" Solution:** Placing cut stems in standing water provides continuous hydraulic capillary replenishment, while the loose plastic cover maintains local micro-humidity around 80–90% without pooling liquid water on delicate leaf tissues.
