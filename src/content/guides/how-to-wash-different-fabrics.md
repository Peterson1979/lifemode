## Comprehensive Fiber Classification & Washing Matrix

| Fabric Category | Fibers Included | Optimal Temperature | Cycle Type | Detergent Type | Drying Method |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cellulose (Plant)** | Cotton, Linen, Hemp | Cold to Warm (30–40°C) | Normal / Regular | Standard Enzyme Detergent | Tumble Dry Low / Medium |
| **Protein (Animal)** | Wool, Cashmere, Alpaca | Cold (Under 25°C) | Wool / Hand Wash | Enzyme-Free pH-Neutral (Wool Wash) | Flat Air Dry on Towel |
| **Fine Filament** | Mulberry Silk, Rayon, Viscose | Cold | Delicates (Mesh Bag) | Gentle Silk Wash | Hang Dry in Shade |
| **Synthetics** | Polyester, Nylon, Acrylic | Cold to Warm | Permanent Press | Standard Liquid | Low Heat Tumble Dry |
| **Elastomers** | Spandex, Lycra, Elastane | Strictly Cold | Delicates | Mild Liquid (No Softeners) | Air Dry (Heat ruins elasticity) |
