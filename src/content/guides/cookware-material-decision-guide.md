## Summary Decision Matrix: How to Choose Your Pan

```
Are you cooking delicate eggs or pancakes?
  └─ YES ──> Ceramic / Non-Stick (8"–10")
  └─ NO
       │
       ├─ Are you searing steaks or baking in the oven?
       │    └─ YES ──> Seasoned Cast Iron (10"–12")
       │
       ├─ Are you stir-frying or flipping food with wrist action?
       │    └─ YES ──> Carbon Steel (Wok / Skillet)
       │
       └─ Are you making pan sauces, tomato reductions, or daily dinners?
            └─ YES ──> Tri-Ply Stainless Steel (10"–12")
```
