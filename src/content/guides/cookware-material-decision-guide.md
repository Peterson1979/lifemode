## Material Science & Heat Distribution

Different cookware alloys excel at specific culinary tasks due to their thermal conductivity and volumetric heat capacity. 

Matching the pan metal to the cooking technique optimizes heat transfer, prevents hot spots, and ensures effortless food release.
