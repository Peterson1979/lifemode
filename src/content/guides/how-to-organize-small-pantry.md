## The Geometry of Spatial Optimization

In small pantries, usable volume is determined by packing density:

$$\text{Usable Volume} = \text{Shelf Depth} \times \text{Shelf Width} \times \text{Shelf Height} \times \text{Packing Fraction}$$

* **Bagged Foods & Round Jars:** Packing fraction $\approx 0.55$ (45% wasted space).
* **Modular Stackable Rectangles:** Packing fraction $\approx 0.90$ (10% wasted space).

By standardizing container footprints and matching container heights to shelf intervals, you effectively double the functional storage capacity of standard cabinetry.
