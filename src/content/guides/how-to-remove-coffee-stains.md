## The Biochemistry of Coffee Stains

Coffee contains over 1,000 distinct chemical compounds. The primary staining agents are **tannins (polyphenolic plant compounds)** and **melanoidins (dark brown polymeric pigments formed via Maillard reactions during bean roasting)**.

By applying mild acetic acid (white distilled vinegar), we alter the local pH and disrupt the hydrogen bonding between tannin molecules and cotton cellulose, allowing complete removal with cool water before thermal fixation occurs.
