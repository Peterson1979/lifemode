## The Biochemistry of Coffee Stains

Coffee contains over 1,000 chemical compounds. The primary staining agents are **tannins (polyphenolic plant compounds)** and **melanoidins (brown polymers formed via Maillard reactions during roasting)**.

```
Coffee Tannins (Polyphenols) + Cellulose Fibers
                     ↓
   Hydrogen Bonding + Van der Waals Forces (Brown Staining)
                     ↓
   Acidic Treatment (Acetic Acid / Vinegar, pH ~2.5) 
                     ↓
   Protonation of Tannin Molecules → Soluble in Water Rinse
```

By applying mild acetic acid (white vinegar), we alter the pH and disrupt the hydrogen bonding between the tannin molecules and cotton cellulose, allowing clean rinsing without fiber degradation.
