## The Optical Physics of Glass Streaks

Glass is an amorphous, non-porous silica solid ($SiO_2$). When optical light hits clean glass, 92% of light transmits directly through.

Streaks are **light scattering artifacts** caused by two factors:
1. **Evaporated Mineral Solids:** Calcium and sodium salts from hard tap water that reflect diffuse light.
2. **Surfactant Residue:** Excess detergent or blue dye binders that create microscopic oily films.

Using distilled water with minimal surfactants and mechanically removing liquid with a squeegee achieves optical clarity.
