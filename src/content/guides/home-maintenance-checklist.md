## Building Science: The Rule of Water Exclusion

In residential engineering, over 90% of structural basement failures, mold growth, insect infestations, and foundation fractures stem from uncontrolled water accumulation. 

Maintaining clear roof drainage and a positive perimeter slope (soil sloping downward away from the foundation at 1 inch per foot for the first 6 feet) is the single most cost-effective maintenance discipline a homeowner can practice.
