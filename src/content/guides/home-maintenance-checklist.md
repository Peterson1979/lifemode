## Building Science: The Rule of Water Exclusion

In residential engineering, 90% of structural failures, rot, insect infestations, and air quality issues stem from uncontrolled water:

```
Roof Runoff → Clean Gutters → Downspouts (Discharged 6ft+ away) → Sloped Grading
                                           ↓
                 Foundation Footing Remains Dry & Structurally Sound
```

Maintaining roof drainage and positive perimeter slope (soil sloping downward away from foundation at 1 inch per foot for the first 6 feet) is the single most valuable maintenance routine a homeowner can perform.
