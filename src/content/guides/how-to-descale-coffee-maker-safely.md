## Understanding Mineral Scale in Coffee Brewers

Tap water contains dissolved calcium and magnesium ions ($Ca^{2+}, Mg^{2+}$). Under repetitive heating inside copper or thermoblock boilers, these ions precipitate out as insoluble calcium carbonate ($CaCO_3$) scale.

Over time, mineral scale restricts water flow, drops brewing temperature below optimal extraction levels (92°C–96°C), and increases electrical pump strain.
