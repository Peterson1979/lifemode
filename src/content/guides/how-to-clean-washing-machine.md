## Why Washing Machines Develop Sour Odors

Modern high-efficiency front-load washing machines use tight rubber door gaskets to create a watertight seal. When cold-water cycles are run repeatedly using excess detergent and liquid fabric softeners, unrinsed surfactants bind with skin cells and sebum to form a gelatinous biofilm (*scrud*) between the inner and outer drums.

Running monthly thermal sanitation cycles (90°C+) and maintaining air circulation eliminates the moisture required for fungal spore germination.
