## Practical workflows for everyday tasks
### Meal planning
1. Open Claude or ChatGPT in your browser or preferred app.
2. Type a simple prompt: *“Plan a vegetarian dinner for four people with a budget of $30.”* 
3. The model returns a menu, ingredient list, and a quick prep timeline. Copy the list into your phone’s notes app and shop directly from there.
### Draft editing
1. Paste a paragraph you’ve written into the chat window.
2. Ask: *“Make this sound more concise and friendly.”* 
3. The AI returns a revised version you can copy back into your document. This works for emails, cover letters, or social‑media posts.
### Schedule organization
1. Export your calendar events to a CSV file or simply list upcoming appointments.
2. Prompt the model: *“Create a weekly plan that blocks time for work, exercise, and family, based on these events.”* 
3. Review the suggested time blocks and adjust as needed – no spreadsheet required.
### Document summarization
1. Upload a PDF or paste text from a long report.
2. Request: *“Summarize the key takeaways in three bullet points.”* 
3. The AI extracts the core ideas, saving you minutes of reading.
These workflows stay within the safe uses outlined by BGR and avoid the prohibited domains like medical advice.
