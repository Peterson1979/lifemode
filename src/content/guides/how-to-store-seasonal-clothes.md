## Why Clothes Moths Attack Natural Fibers

Adult clothes moths have vestigial mouthparts and cannot ingest fabric. It is the microscopic **caterpillars (larvae)** that feed voraciously on keratin protein filaments.

Garments meticulously laundered or dry-cleaned before storage are stripped of attractant human oils. Storing them in sealed, breathable containers creates an impenetrable defense against seasonal infestation.
