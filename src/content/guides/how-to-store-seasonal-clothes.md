## Why Clothes Moths Attack Natural Fibers

Adult clothes moths do not have mouthparts and cannot eat. It is the microscopic **caterpillars (larvae)** that consume protein:

```
Keratin (Wool, Silk, Feathers) + Trace Nitrogen (Sweat/Sebum Residue)
                              ↓
              Enzymatic Digestion by Moth Larvae
                              ↓
                  Holes and Fiber Damage
```

Clean garments stripped of human oils and stored in sealed, breathable containers eliminate the essential nutritional stimulants larvae require to hatch and survive.
