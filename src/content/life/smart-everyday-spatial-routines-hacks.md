Most people attempt to fix chaotic daily routines with sheer willpower. They tell themselves they will simply "try harder" to keep keys organized, prepare morning meals on time, or keep countertops clean.

Yet cognitive psychology consistently shows that **environment always beats willpower**.

When your living space creates subtle physical friction—such as putting essential items behind closed cupboard doors or leaving cords tangled in high-traffic zones—your brain defaults to the path of least resistance.

By applying small, physics-backed spatial hacks to your home, you eliminate daily micro-frustrations before they start.

## The 2-Minute Reset Protocol

At the end of each evening, execute a rapid **2-minute reset** of your main living space:

1. **Clear the Sink:** Wash or load all dinner dishes into the dishwasher.
2. **Reset the Entry Tray:** Ensure keys, wallet, and sunglasses are in the landing zone.
3. **Stage Tomorrow's First Task:** Set out your coffee bean canister, fill your water kettle, and place a clean notebook on your desk.

Waking up to an organized, friction-free living environment immediately sets a calm, focused tone for your entire day.
